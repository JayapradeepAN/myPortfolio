Case study 1 · iPrint dashboard · 2024

One Dashboard, Total Control: Redesigning How Admins Manage Enterprise Printing

OpenText iPrint had no unified view of the print environment. Admins managing printers, print managers, driver stores, and renderers had to move between separate screens to find out what was running, what was down, and what needed attention. With deployments reaching hundreds of thousands of printers, spotting an issue meant hunting for it, and fixing one meant several more steps.

Case study 2 · Data Insights dashboard · 2022

Turning Petabytes of Storage Data into Decisions at a Glance

OES had no single place to see how storage was being used. The information existed, but it was scattered across different UIs: server details in one place, volume details in another, and file and owner data somewhere else. To answer a basic question like “where is my storage going?”, admins had to open several tools and piece the answer together by hand. Nothing showed which data was hot (actively used) and which was cold (rarely touched), so capacity planning and migration decisions were slow and unreliable.

Case study 3 · Create printer · 2021

Cutting Printer Setup Time by 40%: Reimagining How Admins Create Printers in iPrint

IT administrators creating a printer in OES iPrint have to configure many interdependent settings (server, container, print manager, drivers, features, email, LPR) in a rigid, opaque flow. They get no sense of progress, no warning when features conflict, and no way to check their entries before submitting. The result is a slow task (about 18 minutes per printer), frequent post-creation errors, and heavy reliance on external notes and support tickets.
